# Tax Section Daily — data feed

Daily section studies of the **CGST Act, 2017** and the **Income-tax Act, 2025** (Act, interpretation, rules, notifications, case laws, forms, expert commentary, diagrams and charts), plus a digest of new CBIC/CBDT notifications.

- `data.json` — updated every morning (IST) by a scheduled task. The Tax Section Daily Chrome add-on downloads it from
  `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/asgowdakarur/tax-section-daily/main/data.json`

Study aid, not legal advice. Verify statutory text and case status on official portals (cbic.gov.in, incometaxindia.gov.in) before relying on it.
